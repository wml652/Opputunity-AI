### Research Internship in Science & Engineering (RISE) at IITK

Applications invited for RISE @ IITK Winter Virtual Internship 2026

For the application process and link, refer to the [Application Guide for Students](https://www.iitk.ac.in/oir/data/Application-Guide-for-Students-Project-List-16-09-26.pdf)

### About the Program

RISE (Research Internship for International Students) at IITK is an international research internship programme that provides students from around the world with an opportunity to gain hands-on research experience under the mentorship of IIT Kanpur faculty. The programme enables participants to engage in cutting-edge research projects, develop academic and professional skills, network meaningfully with peers from around the world and experience life on an Indian campus.

### Program Period

Winter Internship (Online): In December

Summer Internship (Offline): For 6-8 weeks between May to July

### Tentative Application Period

- Winter internship: September-October
- Summer internship: February-March

### Application Process

- When the application process opens, a list of the available research projects/faculty for both winter and summer internships will be shared on the website.
- Each student can apply for up to two projects through the RISE Application Google form along with the required documents
- The applications will be reviewed by the faculty who may choose to conduct online interviews
- Final results will be conveyed to the selected students by the Office of International Relations
