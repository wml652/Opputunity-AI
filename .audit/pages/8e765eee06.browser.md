The Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA), also known as CBD Punjab, is seeking exceptional minds and talented young professionals for a dynamic learning experience.

The Youth Career Program (YCP) is an internship program designed to provide aspiring professionals with practical exposure, professional grooming and an opportunity to contribute towards the development of Punjab’s future.

Program

The Youth Career Program offers young professionals an internship opportunity to work in a structured and progressive environment while gaining hands-on experience across multiple departments at CBD Punjab.

**Through this program, participants will:**

Gain practical workplace exposure

Learn from experienced professionals

Enhance their professional skills

Experience a dynamic, collaborative work culture

Explore opportunities across multiple directorates

for Applicants

All educational degrees must be from HEC-recognized institutions in Pakistan. Degrees from foreign institutions must be duly issued and preferably verified by HEC, where applicable.

Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interview. The Authority reserves the right to accept or reject any application without assigning a reason.

Shortlisted candidates must bring their original academic credentials and relevant experience certificates or letters at the time of interview.

Candidates must possess an academic or professional qualification relevant to the Directorate for which they are applying.
