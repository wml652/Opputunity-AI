## 5 Tips to Never Miss a Scholarship Deadline

1

#### Use a Deadline Tracker

Add every scholarship deadline to your phone calendar with reminders set 30 days, 14 days, and 3 days before. Or use [College Decider's planning tool](https://www.collegedecider.com/plan) to track everything in one place.

2

#### Start Early — Most Deadlines Are Firm

Unlike college applications, scholarship deadlines are rarely extended. Begin your essays 4-6 weeks before the due date.

3

#### Apply to 10-15 Scholarships Per Season

Diversify your applications. Apply to a mix of large national and small local scholarships — local ones often have fewer applicants and better odds.

4

#### Reuse and Adapt Your Essays

Many scholarship essay prompts overlap. Write a strong core essay and adapt it for different applications. Use [AI Essay Studio](https://www.collegedecider.com/essaystudio) to refine your drafts.

5

#### Check Your Target Schools' Internal Scholarships

Many colleges offer merit scholarships automatically with your application. Others require a separate app. Check each school's financial aid page.
